Overdrive Asset Production · Phase 4

Historical Fidelity Lock — March CG891

2026-08-10 · Mission: MISSION.md · ~45–60 min

The purpose of this phase

Phase 4 converts the approved Overdrive-style candidate into a historically recognizable March CG891 reference before any multi-view or 3D reconstruction work begins.

Style is not identity. A car can look like Overdrive and still be the wrong car. This phase locks the CG891 silhouette and livery logic without copying commercial logos or text.

1. Provided artifacts

  • Clean side reference: design/art/reference/team_tier2_a/historical_clean.png
  • Source montage: design/art/reference/1989-canonical-reference.jpg
  • Style authority: design/art/art-bible.md — Overdrive style means the Fujishima + Matsuri fusion.
  • Vehicle authority: design/assets/specs/team_tier2_a-car-assets.md — team_tier2_a / May / March CG891.

2. What is locked and what is adapted

3. Build the edit graph manually

No workflow file is required. Create the graph from the following contract so the lesson remains reproducible after the old export is removed.

  1. Use design/art/reference/team_tier2_a/historical_clean.png as the clean side reference. Copy it into the ComfyUI input folder. Recommended Windows command: 
    Copy-Item "design/art/reference/team_tier2_a/historical_clean.png" `
  "E:\ComfyUI\ComfyUI-Easy-Install\ComfyUI\input\historical_clean.png" -Force
  2. Create these 13 nodes: UNETLoader, CLIPLoader, VAELoader, LoraLoaderModelOnly, LoadImage, VAEEncode, EmptySD3LatentImage, Krea2EditModelPatch, Krea2EditGroundedEncode, ConditioningZeroOut, KSampler, VAEDecode, and SaveImage.
  3. Connect LoadImage.image to VAEEncode.pixels, Krea2EditModelPatch.source_image, and Krea2EditGroundedEncode.image.
  4. Connect VAELoader.vae to VAEEncode.vae, Krea2EditModelPatch.vae, and VAEDecode.vae. Connect VAEEncode.latent to Krea2EditModelPatch.source_latent. Connect EmptySD3LatentImage.latent to both Krea2EditModelPatch.target_latent and KSampler.latent_image; do not connect raw image or VAE outputs to these ports.
  5. Connect UNETLoader.model to LoraLoaderModelOnly.model, set the LoRA strength to 1.0, connect its model output to Krea2EditModelPatch.model, then connect the patched model to KSampler.model.
  6. Connect CLIPLoader.clip to Krea2EditGroundedEncode.clip, enter the prompt from Section 5 in the encoder, connect its conditioning output to KSampler.positive and to ConditioningZeroOut.conditioning, then connect ConditioningZeroOut.conditioning to KSampler.negative.
  7. Connect KSampler.samples to VAEDecode.samples, VAELoader.vae to VAEDecode.vae, and VAEDecode.image to SaveImage.images.
  8. Do not enable the Krea2T Enhancer. It is intentionally absent from this phase.

5. Prompt used by the graph

anime style, 2D hand-painted anime racing illustration, Overdrive style: Kosuke Fujishima and Hino Matsuri fusion, clean heroic mechanical drafting, delicate expressive linework, vibrant hard-edged cel shading, painted opaque colors, warm golden highlights. Transform the supplied March CG891 side-profile reference into a historical-fidelity Overdrive reference image. Treat the reference silhouette as structural authority: preserve the extremely narrow and low 1989 monocoque, long pointed nose, low open cockpit, no halo, exposed wheels, visible double-wishbone suspension, compact sidepods, compact engine cover, period-correct front wing and large rear wing. Keep a strict left-facing side profile with the entire car centered and visible from nose to rear wing. Keep the livery historically recognizable but adapted for Overdrive: Miami Blue #66C5C5 covers approximately 70 percent of the visible body; restrained deep green #2E7D32 follows the reference accent band and small geometric blocks; matte black or carbon appears only on tires, floor, wings, suspension, and mechanical areas. No broad white body panels. Remove all sponsor names, logos, numbers, letters, watermarks, and readable markings while preserving their geometric placement only as unmarked color shapes. Do not invent modern aerodynamic features, halo bars, oversized sidepods, modern high noses, or unrelated parts. Solid neutral gray background, soft controlled studio lighting, full car in frame, no driver, no helmet, no cropping, no photorealism, no PBR, no normal maps, no gradients.

6. Execute the phase

  1. Queue the manually built graph once without changing any value.
  2. Wait for the image at E:\ComfyUI\ComfyUI-Easy-Install\ComfyUI\output\overdrive\phase4.
  3. Confirm the output is a single complete side-profile car, not a turnaround sheet or a perspective view.
  4. Compare the generated image against the source crop and the Art Bible. Do not compare only against the previous D image; D is a style candidate, not the historical authority.
  5. Record the result using the evidence template below.

Gate: every criterion must pass. If silhouette or livery fails, stop and revise this phase. Do not proceed to multi-view or 3D.
