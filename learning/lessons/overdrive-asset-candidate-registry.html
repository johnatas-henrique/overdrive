Comparison notes (updated 2026-08-16)

  • March CG891 is the approved first test candidate (28k tris, usable UVs, separated wheels, visible cockpit). License is the only open blocker.
  • McLaren MP4/5 does not displace March: 11.7× the triangle cost with no LODs, fused wheels per axle, and non-uniform scale (~1.3–1.7× real). Candidate classification: usable only after heavy preparation.
  • Minardi M186 is the strongest candidate so far: real-F1 proportions (4.55 × 2.35 × 1.09 m), complete AC hierarchy, repaintable UV (AC V-negative tile, +1 V offset), 76k tris (decimate ~35% or structural cut). License and FBX production route pending.
  • Generic F1 1993 is a light contender (29.6k tris, within budget, wheels separated, driver included & removable). Blockers: ~100× scale normalization, generic material names, unknown license (material name references a McLaren MP4/8 texture → possible parts mashup), 1 UV set pending quality check.
  • Zakspeed 891 (AC 1989) is the strongest audited profile: real scale (4.48 m), complete hierarchy, 24 semantic mats, 29.3k tris (no decimation), 2048² livery + official skins. Import needs the systemic alpha/path fix (FBX references .backup; DXT5 alpha treated as transparency). License (AC 1989) pending — the ASR 1991 carset has explicit authorization.
  • Next audits should target the AC 1989 carset (same era family as March, comparable structure) and then the ASR 1991 carset (license already authorized by the asset author — 22.7k tris per Lotus 101, no decimation needed).
