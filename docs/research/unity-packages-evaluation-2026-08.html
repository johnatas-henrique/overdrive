3. Cinemachine — open question (reopen ADR-0010?)

Why ADR-0010 originally said no Cinemachine (docs/architecture/adr-0010-…, lines 17, 42, 248): "No Cinemachine — MVP uses basic Unity Camera + custom C# camera controller"; "simple Transform lerp — lower learning curve, smaller build, no version risk."

What the prototype revealed (2026-08-01): hand-rolling smooth follow on top of 60 Hz discrete physics ticks produced visible jitter through several fix rounds (double smoothing, LateUpdate ordering, interpolation races). This is precisely the problem Cinemachine solves natively (follow damping, blends, noise, look-ahead).

  • Production camera spec (design/gdd/camera.md) has 4 modes: Chase, Cockpit, PitCamera, Finished — with blends. That is Cinemachine's core use case.
  • Decision point: install Cinemachine and amend ADR-0010, or keep custom camera and budget the engineering time. Not decided yet — user approval required. Reopen when production camera work starts.
