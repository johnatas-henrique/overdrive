## Track Identity

Monaco in 1989 was nearly the modern layout. Key differences: Nouvelle Chicane was tight and concrete-edged, **pit lane had temporary modular structures** (pre-2004 renovation — see correction below), Loews (Fairmont) Hotel had just been built over the tunnel (1973). The circuit was a street course through Monte Carlo and La Condamine around Port Hercule.

**Per-track trackside objects** listed below are in ADDITION to shared assets (ASSET-101 barriers, ASSET-103 grandstands, etc.).

---

## Pit Building (1989 era — Temporary Modular)

**⚠️ IMPORTANT CORRECTION:** In 1989, Monaco did NOT have a permanent pit building. The pits were **temporary modular metal structures** erected for race weekend and dismantled after, located on the harbor straight (Port Hercule). The permanent pit building came post-2004. The "back-to-front" garage configuration (pits facing away from track) is also post-2004. For our game set in 1991, Monaco uses the 1973-era temporary modular configuration.

## Key Visual References

- Casino Square: iconic tight corner through square with Casino, Hotel de Paris, Café de Paris
- Tunnel: 77m long, exit under Fairmont Hotel, emerging into daylight at Nouvelle Chicane
- Harbor: the only circuit with water alongside the pit straight — unique visual identity
- Tight barriers: walls are millimeters from the track everywhere
